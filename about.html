About YewForge

We build Nivren, and we show our working.

YewForge is an independent UK technology company building Nivren: personal intelligence you can run, inspect and trust, on hardware people already own.

What we do

One platform, several ways in.

Nivren is in active development. Nivren Home (NivDroid) is an internal test build, with no public build available. NivKode is our developer environment, with public launch scheduled for 2 November 2026; no public build is available before then. NivMac remains a direction with no public build. Nivren Quark is planned.

The aim is for Nivren to develop more intelligence of its own over time, while still using the best external models where they are the better tool, and always saying which is which.

How we build

Three habits we hold ourselves to.

Preregister first

Method

We write down what an experiment will test, and how it will be judged, before we collect any data.

Keep every result

Method

We record results whatever they show. Inconclusive and negative findings are kept alongside the positive ones, not buried.

Seal the checks

Method

Hidden test checks are designed to stay sealed, and to be read once, only after the collected data has been validated.

Model provenance

Every model carries one of three labels.

We never blur these categories for marketing. If a model is not ours, we say so.

Nivren-native

Model work YewForge has produced and owns.

Nivren-derived

Built by YewForge on top of an upstream model, such as an adapter or fine-tune, and released under that model's licence. The upstream model is always named.

Nivren-integrated

An external model or service that Nivren can use. YewForge does not claim it.
