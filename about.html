About YewForge

Nivren: work in progress, evidence in view.

YewForge is UK-based, reaching globally, and is building Nivren: personal intelligence you can run, inspect and trust, on hardware people already own.

Current work

One platform, several ways in.

Nivren is in active development. Nivren Home (NivDroid) is an internal test build. NivKode 0.1.0 is a review candidate; release approval and publication are pending. Its launch target is 2 November 2026 and may move if testing finds issues. NivMac remains a direction with no public build; Nivren Quark is planned.

The aim is for Nivren to develop more intelligence of its own over time, while still using the best external models where they are the better tool, and always saying which is which.

Research practice

Three evidence rules.

Preregister first

Method

Experiments state their question and evaluation method before data collection.

Keep every result

Method

Results are recorded whether positive, negative or inconclusive.

Seal the checks

Method

Hidden test checks are designed to stay sealed, and to be read once, only after the collected data has been validated.

Model provenance

Every model carries one of three labels.

These categories distinguish original work, derivatives and external services.

Nivren-native

Model work YewForge has produced and owns.

Nivren-derived

Built by YewForge on top of an upstream model, such as an adapter or fine-tune, and released under that model's licence. The upstream model is always named.

Nivren-integrated

An external model or service that Nivren can use. YewForge does not claim it.
