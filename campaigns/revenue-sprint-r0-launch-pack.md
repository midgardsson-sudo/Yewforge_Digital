# Cadence after launch
Support should be a quiet footer to real work, not the main content.

**Default rule:** roughly nine useful/project posts for every one explicit support-led post.

Good moments to mention support:
- a public release;
- a benchmark/evaluation result;
- an Inconvenience issue;
- a transparent 'what £X enabled' receipt;
- a major hardware/compute experiment;
- a real milestone toward the first worker or Nivren Lab.

Bad moments:
- repeating the same appeal without new work;
- inserting a funding pitch into unrelated conversations;
- creating artificial urgency;
- implying supporters receive investment rights or guaranteed future products.
