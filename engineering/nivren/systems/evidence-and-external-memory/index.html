Evidence and External Memory

A record discipline for preserving context, provenance and decisions across long-running engineering work. Memory is not treated as proof, and documentation is not treated as implementation evidence by itself.

  • Status: Current
  • Last reviewed: 3 October 2026
  • Evidence classes: IMPLEMENTED · VALIDATED · HISTORICAL

Purpose and public status

We keep durable records of engineering decisions and validation so work can resume with its context. Our 3 October review confirms these practices in bounded implementations.

Public evidence and limits

Claim: Evidence and lineage are explicit engineering artefacts in Nivren's documented workflow.

Result: Evidence records and recovery behaviour were validated in bounded implementations.

Limit: These results do not show that every product component shares complete or error-free memory.
