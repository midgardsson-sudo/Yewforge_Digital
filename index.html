Evidence

Most experiments didn’t work. We kept them.

Each experiment was preregistered before data was collected, and hidden checks were sealed until collection was validated. These are the latest outcomes, as recorded.

INCONCLUSIVEInternal pilot

PyOrgan v3: repair strategies

all four arms 14/24
organ vs generic: 0 net wins

No difference. The draft was unchanged by the repair in 10 of 14 failing cases, which pointed to the next question.

REJECTInternal pilot

Reflex R0: learned routing

learned 17/24 @ 2.42 calls
simple rule 16/24 @ 2.33 calls

A learned routing policy did not beat a simple rule on the preregistered cost condition.

NOT CONFIRMEDExploratory

Must-change repair framing

changed 8/14 failing drafts vs 2/14
6/6 discordant pairs · Holm p = 0.031

Measures revision rate, not correctness, on one 3B model (n = 14). A preregistered confirmatory test on a fresh suite did not confirm this exploratory result.

Provenance

We say which models are ours.

Every model we mention carries one of three labels. We don’t blur them for marketing.

  • Nivren-native

    Model work genuinely produced and owned by Nivren.

  • Nivren-derived

    Adapters, fine-tunes and derivatives of upstream models, under their licences.

  • Nivren-integrated

    External models and services Nivren routes to, and does not claim to own.
