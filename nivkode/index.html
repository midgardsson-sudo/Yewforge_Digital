We are developing NivKode as a developer environment.

For developers who want to choose, for each conversation, between a local model and an external AI provider they sign in to.

Launch target: Friday 13 November 2026; may move if testing finds issues.

Get the launch note See the evidence yewforge.partnerships@gmail.com

Desktop, command-line and terminal interfaces remain development goals. In the desktop app, new conversations start on a local model when one is installed. Defaults in other clients vary. When a conversation uses Codex, NivKode sends that conversation to the provider of your signed-in Codex account (for example OpenAI or Amazon Bedrock), under your own account.
