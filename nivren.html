Updates

One identity. Many specialist models. A human with the final say.

  • Desktop local start

    In the NivKode desktop app, new conversations start on a local model when one is installed. Defaults in other clients vary.

  • Governed, not autonomous

    Models and tools are bounded contributors under one governed environment. Consequential actions need your approval.

  • Inspectable

    Memory, evidence and system state are records you can look at, not things hidden behind a chat window.

  • More than chat

    Conversation is one possible interface. Other directions remain at different stages of development and are not public releases.

Three labels, never blurred.

Nivren will grow more intelligence of its own over time and still use other excellent models where they are the better tool. Every model it uses carries one of these.

  • Nivren-native. Model work genuinely produced and owned by Nivren.
  • Nivren-derived. Adapters, fine-tunes and derivatives of upstream models, under their licences.
  • Nivren-integrated. External models and services Nivren routes to, and does not claim to own.
