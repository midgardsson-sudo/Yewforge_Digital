What Nivren is

One identity. Many specialist models. A human with the final say.

Principle 1

Local-first

Designed to run on ordinary local hardware. Cloud convenience is used where it helps; it is not the product’s identity.

Principle 2

Governed, not autonomous

Models and tools are bounded contributors under one governed environment. Consequential actions need your approval.

Principle 3

Inspectable

Memory, evidence and system state are records you can look at, not things hidden behind a chat window.

Principle 4

More than chat

Conversation is one doorway. Nivren also reaches you through NivKode, NivDroid and NivMac.

Model provenance

Three labels, never blurred.

Nivren will grow more intelligence of its own over time and still use other excellent models where they are the better tool. Every model it uses carries one of these.

  • Nivren-native

    Model work genuinely produced and owned by Nivren.

  • Nivren-derived

    Adapters, fine-tunes and derivatives of upstream models, under their licences.

  • Nivren-integrated

    External models and services Nivren routes to, and does not claim to own.
