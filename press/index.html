What each product is, and where it stands.

  • NivKode

    0.1.0 · review candidate

    NivKode 0.1.0 is a review candidate and remains in testing. Release approval and publication are pending. The launch target is Friday 13 November 2026 and may move if testing finds issues.

  • Nivren

    In active development

    We are developing Nivren to coordinate specialist models, memory, tools and applications. These are design goals; broader integrated behaviour is still planned.

  • Nivren Home (NivDroid)

    Internal test build

    This is an internal test build. No public build is available.

  • NivDroid

    In development

    NivDroid is an Android OS in development.

  • NivMac

    Direction; no public build

    A lightweight, network-connected OS direction for capable older computers. No public build is available.

  • Nivren Quark

    Planned

    A planned family of small local models in three sizes: NQ-0.5B, NQ-1B and NQ-3B. Each size will carry its provenance label when it is released.
