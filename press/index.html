Descriptions

YewForge builds Nivren: personal intelligence you can run, inspect and trust.
One-liner
YewForge is an independent UK technology company building Nivren, an AI platform designed to run on hardware people already own. We record what our evidence shows, including when it shows nothing, and we keep a clear line between models we build and models we use.
YewForge, short
Nivren is personal intelligence from YewForge, in active development. It works with local and external models and tells you which model is which. Nivren Home (NivDroid) is an internal test build. NivKode is our developer environment, with public launch scheduled for 2 November 2026 and no public build available before then. NivMac is a direction with no public build; Nivren Quark is planned.

Claims and caveats

What we can say, and what we can't yet.

We only make claims we can evidence. Each one below comes with the limit that travels with it.

Preregistration

Evidenced

We preregister experiments before collecting data, and we record results whatever they show.

CaveatThis is our practice, not an outside review. It is not peer-reviewed.

Sealed checks

Evidenced

Hidden test checks are designed to be sealed and read once, only after the collected data is validated.

CaveatSome schema and digest inspection has happened before scoring. We disclose that rather than claim a perfect seal.

Model labels

Policy

Every model is labelled Nivren-native, Nivren-derived or Nivren-integrated.

CaveatThis is a policy commitment; each public model page must apply it.

Runs on a phone

Internal only

Internal review builds of the Nivren launcher run on an Android test phone.

CaveatOne test device, internal builds. Not a public release.

Claims we don't make

  • No benchmark, speed or accuracy figures. Our recent internal experiments were mostly inconclusive. We'll share results when they meet our own evidence bar.
  • No "on-device only" or privacy guarantees. Nivren can route to external models, and says so.
  • No user numbers, testimonials or "as featured in". There are none yet, and we won't invent them.
