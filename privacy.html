Privacy

Privacy information.

This notice explains how YewForge Digital handles personal information provided through the website or by email.

This website

This website does not use forms, analytics or advertising trackers to collect information. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical hosting logs, such as visitor and request information, to provide and secure the service; GitHub controls those logs. See GitHub's information about data collection for Pages and its Privacy Statement.

Nivren and NivKode

In the NivKode desktop app, new conversations start on a local model when one is installed. When a conversation uses Codex, NivKode sends that conversation to the provider of your signed-in Codex account (for example OpenAI or Amazon Bedrock), under your own account.

Email and social profiles

An email enquiry provides the sender address and any information included in the message. The information is used to respond and handle the enquiry, and correspondence is kept only as long as needed for that purpose or related legal requirements. Our email is hosted by Google (Gmail). Requests about information held can be sent to yewforge.partnerships@gmail.com.

Social profiles are hosted by third-party platforms. Those platforms may process information under their own privacy notices and settings; YewForge does not control their data handling.

Support through Ko-fi

If you support us through Ko-fi, Ko-fi and its payment providers handle the payment under their own privacy notices. We receive only the supporter details that Ko-fi shares with us, and we use them to record and acknowledge support and to keep the financial records the law requires.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have the right to request access to, correction or deletion of your personal information, to restrict or object to its use, or to receive information you provided in a portable format. Where processing relies on consent, that consent can be withdrawn. Requests can be sent to the email above. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

About this notice

This short notice was prepared using the ICO's free guidance for small organisations. This notice should be reviewed if services or information handling change.
