Why support matters

Small support can add real capacity.

At our current scale, even modest support can directly fund another development tool, additional model/API headroom, testing infrastructure or part of the hardware fund.

We are not asking supporters to fund a founder salary. The purpose of this channel is to help the work sustain and accelerate itself.

First public goal

£50 — fund additional development capacity.

Start small. Show exactly what it enabled.

The first £50 net received through this support channel will be assigned to additional development capacity: coding-agent access, model/API credits or equivalent tooling that can immediately increase productive throughput.

When a milestone is reached, YewForge intends to record what the support enabled. Future goals may include £100, £250, £500, the first dedicated 24GB GPU worker and, later, the Nivren compute lab.

Where it goes

Back into the work.

Typical uses include AI development subscriptions and credits, local and hosted compute, CI and infrastructure, storage, networking, testing devices and the dedicated Nivren hardware fund.

Support is deliberately separated from editorial and technical judgement. Spending can increase capacity; it cannot purchase a favourable conclusion, review or research result.

Please read before offering equipment

Hardware donation terms.

Donated equipment may be opened, dismantled, analysed, modified, repaired, repurposed, upcycled, used for component harvesting or responsibly recycled.

Please do not expect donated equipment to be returned unchanged, or returned at all, unless we separately agree that with you in advance.

Independence

Support never buys a result.

Financial support, compute sponsorship and hardware donations do not buy favourable results, coverage or conclusions. Supporters do not get to choose what our research finds or how we report it.

We apply the same evidence standards whatever the result, including when findings are negative or inconclusive.
