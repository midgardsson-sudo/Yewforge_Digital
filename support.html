Support YewForge

Throw some fuel into the forge.

Support is intended to expand development capacity for Nivren, NivKode and related work through tools, compute, test hardware and infrastructure. It does not influence research conclusions.

Financial support is available through Ko-fi.

Why support matters

Small support can add practical capacity.

Support is not payment for unreleased work or a promise of a particular product, result or financial return. Support does not buy influence over technical or research conclusions.

The first milestone is £50 net. When reached, a public note will describe what the support enabled.

First milestone

£50 net for additional development capacity.

Support is intended to be reinvested in practical development needs such as:

  • additional AI worker capacity and model/API access;
  • compute and development tools;
  • test hardware and infrastructure.

When the milestone is reached, a public note will report what it enabled. The target does not promise a particular service, result or delivery date.

Please read before offering equipment

Hardware donation terms.

Donated equipment may be opened, dismantled, analysed, modified, repaired, repurposed, upcycled, used for component harvesting or responsibly recycled.

Please securely wipe all personal data before donating; YewForge does not accept responsibility for data left on devices.

Donations by arrangement only.

Donated equipment may not be returned or may be returned changed unless a return arrangement is agreed in advance.

Independence

Support never buys a result.

Financial support, compute sponsorship and hardware donations do not buy favourable results, coverage or conclusions. Supporters do not choose research outcomes or reporting.

The same evidence standards apply to positive, negative and inconclusive findings.
