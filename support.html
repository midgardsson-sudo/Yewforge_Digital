Support YewForge

More room to test, learn and build.

Support expands our testing and research capacity. It does not determine what our evidence says.

Why support matters

Capacity changes what we can examine.

Funding, compute and donated hardware give us more time and resources to test Nivren across a broader range of setups, investigate failures and pursue research questions carefully.

More capacity can broaden the work. It cannot predetermine a result.

Please read before offering equipment

Hardware donation terms.

Donated equipment may be opened, dismantled, analysed, modified, repaired, repurposed, upcycled, used for component harvesting or responsibly recycled.

Please do not expect donated equipment to be returned unchanged, or returned at all, unless we separately agree that with you in advance.

Independence

Support never buys a result.

Financial support, compute sponsorship and hardware donations do not buy favourable results, coverage or conclusions. Supporters do not get to choose what our research finds or how we report it.

We apply the same evidence standards whatever the result, including when findings are negative or inconclusive.
